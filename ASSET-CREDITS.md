# Asset credits

Generated by `npm run credits` from the checked-in asset manifest.

The supplied Sharklication logo is the owner's approved mascot artwork, not a wildlife photograph. Its original authorship/reuse terms were not independently established; owner-supplied use is assumed. The 12 fantasy illustrations are original SVG artwork authored for this project (see `src/shark-art.tsx`), not representations of real species.

Each resized photograph remains available under its own source license. Share-alike applies to those image adaptations, not automatically to the application code. Required credit, source links, modification notice, and license are also displayed in the app and cached offline.

The Home Screen/PWA icon is a separate owner-supplied illustration (`assets/app-icon-source.png`), not a wildlife photograph. `scripts/prepare-icons.mjs` only resizes and optimizes it into opaque PNGs, without cropping or redesign. Original authorship/reuse terms were not independently established; owner-supplied use is assumed.

The transparent home-link wordmark was supplied by the owner (`assets/header-logo-source.png`) and resized to WebP after trimming transparent margins, without redesign. Gobfrey is the owner's chosen name for the illustrated whale-shark guide; no third-party toy artwork or affiliation is claimed. Feeding Time's cartoon sharks and fish are original SVG illustrations in `src/feeding-art.tsx`, not wildlife photographs.

Teacher Gobfrey was supplied by the owner (`assets/gobfrey-teacher-source.png`). `npm run teacher` trims only transparent margins and resizes to a 640-pixel-wide WebP for offline help sidebars, without redesign. Owner-supplied use is assumed; original authorship/reuse terms were not independently established. The growing aquariums, named ocean animals and navigation icons are original SVG illustrations in `src/growing-reef.tsx`, `src/ocean-animal.tsx` and `src/nav-icon.tsx`, not photographs or accurate species identifications.
