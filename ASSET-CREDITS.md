# Asset credits

Generated by `npm run credits` from the checked-in asset manifest.

The supplied Sharklication logo is the owner's approved mascot artwork, not a wildlife photograph. Its original authorship/reuse terms were not independently established; owner-supplied use is assumed. The 12 fantasy illustrations are original SVG artwork authored for this project (see `src/shark-art.tsx`), not representations of real species.

Each resized photograph remains available under its own source license. Share-alike applies to those image adaptations, not automatically to the application code. Required credit, source links, modification notice, and license are also displayed in the app and cached offline.
